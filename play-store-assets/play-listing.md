## Full description (English)

```
কই যাবো (Koy Jabo) — "Where will you go?" — is Bangladesh's complete transport companion. Find the best way to travel anywhere in the country, in Bangla or English.

Features:
🚌 2,400+ Dhaka bus routes with live fare, stops and timing
🚇 Dhaka Metro Rail (MRT-6) live status, stations and fare
🚆 Nationwide train schedule and intercity routes
⛴️ Launch and ferry routes with fares
✈️ Domestic flight search
🧭 Offline maps and route directions (OSRM)
🗺️ Interactive map with live bus locations
🤖 AI travel assistant — ask any travel question in Bangla
💰 Fare calculator across all transport modes
⭐ Favorites, search history and offline access

Works fully OFFLINE — routes, fares and maps are cached on your device. Your searches and favorites stay private on your phone.

Available in Bangla and English. Built for Bangladesh.
```
