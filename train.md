# Bangladesh Railway (বাংলাদেশ রেলওয়ে) Complete Train Guide

Bangladesh Railway operates a 3,000+ kilometer network across Eastern and Western railway zones, divided by the Jamuna / Padma river systems and connected via the Bangabandhu Jamuna Bridge and Padma Bridge Rail Link.

## Network Scale & Infrastructure
- **Total Railway Stations**: 440+ stations across 8 administrative divisions
- **Total Intercity & Commuter Trains Tracked**: 255 active trains
- **Major Terminals**: Kamalapur Railway Station (Dhaka), Dhaka Airport, Chattogram Station, Sylhet Station, Rajshahi Station.

## Travel Seat Classes
- **AC_B (Snigdha / স্নিগ্ধা)**: Air-conditioned chair car.
- **AC_S (AC Berth / কেবিন সিট)**: Air-conditioned sleeper berth.
- **SNIGDHA**: First-class AC chair.
- **S_CHAIR (Shovon Chair / শোভন চেয়ার)**: Non-AC standard 2x2 cushioned seating (most popular).
- **SHOVON**: Non-AC economy seating.
