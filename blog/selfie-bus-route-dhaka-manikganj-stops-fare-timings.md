Selfie Paribahan (সেলফি পরিবহন) is the most popular, fast, and reliable semi-sitting bus service connecting Dhaka (Gabtoli) with Hemayetpur, Savar, Nobinagar, Manikganj, and Paturia Ghat. Known for prompt departures, courteous staff, and comfortable seating, Selfie is the primary choice for daily commuters, students, and passengers heading toward the southern districts via Paturia Ferry Terminal.

In this guide, KoyJabo brings you the verified 2026 route stops, fare breakdown, operating hours, and counter details for Selfie Paribahan.

---

## 📍 Selfie Paribahan Complete Route & Stoppages

Selfie operates along the Dhaka-Aricha National Highway (N5), providing rapid connectivity between capital Dhaka and the greater Manikganj district.

### Key Route Stops:
1. **Gabtoli Bus Terminal** (Dhaka Starting Point)
2. **Parbat Cinema Hall**
3. **Amin Bazar**
4. **Modhumoti Model Town**
5. **Boliarpur**
6. **Hemayetpur** (Savar)
7. **Savar Bus Stand** (Bazar)
8. **Nobinagar Smrity Soudho Moor**
9. **Bannabari / Joypara Crossing**
10. **Golra Bus Stand**
11. **Tara Bridge**
12. **Manikganj Bus Stand** (Manikganj City)
13. **Tero Shree Crossing**
14. **Utholi Bus Stand**
15. **Paturia Ferry Ghat** (Terminal Point)

> 💡 **Pro-Tip**: Selfie buses maintain express movement with designated pick-and-drop counters. Unlike local slow buses, they do not loiter on roadside intersections.

## ⏰ Service Hours & Frequency

- **First Bus from Gabtoli**: 6:00 AM
- **Last Bus from Gabtoli**: 10:30 PM
- **First Bus from Paturia Ghat**: 5:30 AM
- **Last Bus from Paturia Ghat**: 10:00 PM
- **Frequency**: Every 10 to 15 minutes during peak commuter hours; every 20 minutes during mid-day.

---

## 🏢 Major Counter Locations & Contacts

- **Gabtoli Counter**: Counter #14 & #15, Opposite Gabtoli Interdistrict Bus Terminal, Mirpur Road.
- **Savar Counter**: Savar Bus Stand Overbridge, N5 Highway.
- **Hemayetpur Counter**: Hemayetpur Chowrasta, Savar.
- **Manikganj Counter**: Manikganj Central Bus Terminal Counter.
- **Paturia Counter**: Ghat #3, Paturia Ferry Terminal.
