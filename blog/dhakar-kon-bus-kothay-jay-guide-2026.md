## 📍 1. From Mirpur (মিরপুর জোন)
Where do you want to go from Mirpur (Mirpur 1, 2, 10, 11, 12)?

- **To Motijheel & Gulistan**: Shikor, Trans Silva, Bikash Paribahan, Dishari, Kalsi Express.
- **To New Market & Azimpur**: Bihanga, Super Bus, Shadhin Paribahan.
- **To Uttara & Airport**: Basumati Transport, Prajapati Paribahan, Alif Paribahan (via Mirpur 14/ECB Chattar).
- **To Badda & Kuril**: Raida Paribahan, Akash Paribahan, Al-Makkah.
- **To Jatrabari & Sayedabad**: Shikor, Trans Silva, Mirpur Link.

## 📍 2. From Uttara & Airport (উত্তরা ও এয়ারপোর্ট জোন)
Where do you want to go from Uttara, Airport, or Abdullahpur?

- **To Mohakhali, Farmgate & Shahbagh**: Victor Classic, Provati Banasree, Balaka, Skybus, BRTC Articulated.
- **To Gulistan & Sadarghat**: Victor Classic, Ajmeri Glory, Suprobhat, Gazipur Paribahan.
- **To Rampura, Badda & Sayedabad**: Raida Paribahan, Anabil Super, Turag Paribahan.
- **To Mirpur & Gabtoli**: Basumati, Prajapati (via ECB Chattar).

## 📍 3. From Gabtoli & Savar (গাবতলী ও সাভার জোন)
Where do you want to go from Gabtoli, Hemayetpur, or Savar?

- **To Manikganj & Paturia**: Selfie Paribahan, Nilachal Paribahan, SB Link.
- **To Farmgate, Shahbagh & Paltan**: Labbaik, Baishakhi, Romjan Paribahan, Gabtoli Link.
- **To Dhanmondi & New Market**: D-Link, Thikana Express, City Bus.
- **To Jatrabari & Narayanganj**: Labbaik, Baishakhi, Thikana Express.

## 📍 4. From Mohammadpur (মোহাম্মদপুর জোন)
Where do you want to go from Mohammadpur, Shankar, or Shia Mosque?

- **To Motijheel & Khilgaon**: Midline Paribahan, Raja City, Moitree Paribahan.
- **To Kuril & Notun Bazar**: Basumati Transport, Taranga Plus.

## 📍 5. From Old Dhaka & Sadarghat (পুরান ঢাকা ও সদরঘাট)
Where do you want to go from Sadarghat, Victoria Park, or Jagannath University?

- **To Uttara & Gazipur**: Victor Classic, Ajmeri Glory.
- **To Mirpur**: Tanjil Paribahan, Bihanga.
- **To Savar & Gabtoli**: D-Link, Moitree.
- **To Dhanmondi**: Moitree Paribahan, City Bus.

## 💵 Official Local Bus Fare Rules in Dhaka 2026

- **Minimum Fare**: ৳10 (Ten Taka) for city buses.
- **Per Kilometer Rate**: ~৳2.45 per km as approved by BRTA.
- **Student Concession (Half Pass)**: 50% discount on regular fare upon showing a valid student ID card on city routes.
