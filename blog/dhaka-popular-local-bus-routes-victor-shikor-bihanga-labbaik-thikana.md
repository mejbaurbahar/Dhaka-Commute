Dhaka's bustling streets move millions of commuters daily through an intricate network of local and semi-sitting buses. For newcomers and daily commuters alike, understanding which bus goes through which major intersection can save hours of transit time.

Based on real commuter search queries on Google Search Console and KoyJabo route telemetry, here is the comprehensive guide to Dhaka's 5 highest-traffic bus services: Victor Classic, Shikor Paribahan, Bihanga, Labbaik, and Ajmeri Glory.

---

## 🚌 1. Victor Classic (ভিক্টর ক্লাসিক)
**Route: Sadarghat ⇄ Abdullahpur (Uttara)**

Victor Classic is the backbone of north-south travel in Dhaka, connecting Old Dhaka's Sadarghat river port with Uttara and Tongi.

- **Key Stops**: Sadarghat → Ray Saheb Bazar → Naya Bazar → Gulistan → Paltan → Kakrail → Shantinagar → Malibagh → Mouchak → Mogbazar → Nabisco → Mohakhali → Banani → Kakali → Airport → Uttara (House Building) → Abdullahpur.
- **Operating Hours**: 6:00 AM – 11:00 PM
- **Average Fare**: Minimum ৳10; Full Route ~৳60.
- **Best For**: Commuters traveling between Sadarghat/Gulistan and Mohakhali/Airport/Uttara without changing buses.

---

## 🚌 2. Shikor Paribahan (শিকড় পরিবহন)
**Route: Mirpur 12 ⇄ Jatrabari**

Shikor Paribahan provides direct transit between Mirpur and southeast Dhaka, bypassing heavy eastern traffic through Farmgate and Shahbagh.

- **Key Stops**: Mirpur 12 → Mirpur 11 → Mirpur 10 → Kazipara → Shewrapara → Agargaon → Khamar Bari → Farmgate → Kawran Bazar → Shahbagh → Matsya Bhaban → Press Club → Paltan → Gulistan → Sayedabad → Jatrabari.
- **Operating Hours**: 6:00 AM – 10:00 PM
- **Average Fare**: Minimum ৳10; Full Route ~৳45.
- **Best For**: Direct travel from Mirpur to Motijheel, Gulistan, and Jatrabari.

---

## 🚌 3. Bihanga (বিহঙ্গ পরিবহন)
**Route: Mirpur 12 ⇄ Azimpur**

Bihanga is the primary academic corridor bus, connecting the educational hubs of Mirpur, Shyamoli, Dhanmondi, Science Lab, New Market, Dhaka University, and Eden College.

- **Key Stops**: Mirpur 12 → Mirpur 10 → Mirpur 1 → Technical → Kallyanpur → Shyamoli → Shishu Mela → Asad Gate → Dhanmondi 27 → Dhanmondi 32 → Shukrabad → Kalabagan → City College → Science Lab → Nilkhet → New Market → Azimpur.
- **Operating Hours**: 6:00 AM – 11:00 PM
- **Average Fare**: Minimum ৳10; Full Route ~৳35.
- **Best For**: Students and shoppers visiting New Market, Dhanmondi, and Dhaka University.

---

## 🚌 4. Labbaik (লাব্বাইক পরিবহন)
**Route: Savar ⇄ Signboard (Narayanganj Link)**

Covering almost 40 kilometers across Dhaka from west to southeast, Labbaik is one of the longest single-ticket cross-city routes in the capital.

- **Key Stops**: Savar → Hemayetpur → Gabtoli → Technical → Shyamoli → Asad Gate → Farmgate → Bangla Motor → Mogbazar → Mouchak → Malibagh → Khilgaon → Kamalapur → Sayedabad → Jatrabari → Rayerbagh → Signboard.
- **Operating Hours**: 6:00 AM – 10:30 PM
- **Average Fare**: Minimum ৳10; Full Route ~৳70.
- **Best For**: Commuters traveling between Savar/Gabtoli and Jatrabari/Signboard via central Dhaka.

---

## 🚌 5. Ajmeri Glory (আজমেরী গ্লোরী)
**Route: Sadarghat ⇄ Chandra (Gazipur)**

Ajmeri Glory is a massive inter-district commuter service connecting Sadarghat directly with the industrial zones of Gazipur, Konabari, and Chandra.

- **Key Stops**: Sadarghat → Gulistan → Paltan → Kakrail → Mogbazar → Mohakhali → Banani → Airport → Uttara → Gazipur Chowrasta → Konabari → Chandra.
- **Operating Hours**: 5:30 AM – 11:00 PM
- **Average Fare**: Minimum ৳10; Full Route ~৳120.
- **Best For**: Workers and traders traveling between Old Dhaka and Gazipur industrial belt.
