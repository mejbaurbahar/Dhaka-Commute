## 🚌 Direct Buses from Gulistan to Dhanmondi

There are several reliable bus services running directly between Gulistan and Dhanmondi:

### 1. Midline Paribahan (মিডলাইন পরিবহন) — Highest Frequency
- **Route**: Khilgaon ⇄ Gulistan ⇄ Mohammadpur
- **Stoppages between Gulistan & Dhanmondi**:
  - Gulistan (Golap Shah Mazar / GPO)
  - Paltan
  - Press Club / High Court
  - Matsya Bhaban
  - Shahbagh
  - Katabon
  - Science Lab
  - City College (Dhanmondi entrance)
  - Jigatola
  - Dhanmondi 15
  - Shankar (Mohammadpur)
- **Fare**: ৳15 to ৳20
- **Travel Time**: 25 – 35 minutes (Non-peak); 45 minutes (Peak hours)

### 2. Thikana Express (ঠিকানা এক্সপ্রেস)
- **Route**: Shonbari/Jatrabari ⇄ Gulistan ⇄ Dhanmondi ⇄ Chandra
- **Dhanmondi Stops**: City College, Kalabagan, Dhanmondi 32, Dhanmondi 27, Asad Gate.
- **Fare**: ৳20 to ৳25
- **Best For**: Passengers going to Dhanmondi 27 and 32 (Mirpur Road side).

### 3. BRTC AC City Bus
- **Stops**: Gulistan (Zero Point) → Shahbagh → Science Lab → Dhanmondi 27.
- **Fare**: ৳30
- **Best For**: Air-conditioned comfort during hot summer afternoons.

## 📍 Where to Board at Gulistan?

1. **Golap Shah Mazar Mor**: Catch Midline and local buses heading towards Science Lab.
2. **GPO Mor / Zero Point**: Board BRTC AC and express services.
3. **Fulbaria Bus Stand**: Alternative boarding spot for connecting services.

---

## 🧭 Return Route: Dhanmondi to Gulistan

Returning from Dhanmondi to Gulistan follows the reverse route along Mirpur Road and Kazi Nazrul Islam Avenue:
- Board **Midline Paribahan** at Dhanmondi 15, Jigatola, or City College.
- Board **Thikana Express** at Dhanmondi 27, 32, or Kalabagan.
- Drop-off at GPO, Paltan, or Gulistan Golap Shah Mazar.
