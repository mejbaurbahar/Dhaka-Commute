The Dhaka-Mawa-Bhanga Expressway (Bangabandhu Sheikh Mujibur Rahman Expressway, N8) is Bangladesh's premier high-speed highway. Millions of tourists, food lovers heading to Mawa for fresh Hilsa fish, and passengers traveling across the Padma Bridge rely on regular direct bus services departing from Dhaka.

Here is the complete commuter guide for traveling from Dhaka (Gulistan, Jatrabari, Sayedabad) to Mawa Ghat and Padma Bridge in 2026.

---

## 🚌 Major Bus Services: Dhaka to Mawa Ghat

Several dedicated bus operators run nonstop and semi-express services from Dhaka along the expressway:

### 1. Gangchil Paribahan (গাংচিল পরিবহন)
- **Boarding Point**: Gulistan (Golap Shah Mazar / Phulbaria Terminal).
- **Route**: Gulistan → Jatrabari / Postagola → Dhaleshwari Toll Plaza → Shreenagar → Mawa Ghat.
- **Type**: Semi-Sitting.
- **Fare**: ৳80 – ৳100.
- **Frequency**: Every 10 minutes from 5:30 AM to 11:00 PM.

### 2. Ilish Paribahan (ইলিশ পরিবহন)
- **Boarding Point**: Gulistan & Sayedabad.
- **Route**: Direct via Mayor Hanif Flyover & Expressway to Mawa & Padma Bridge Toll Plaza.
- **Fare**: ৳90 – ৳110.

### 3. BRTC AC & Non-AC Bus Service
- **Boarding Point**: Gulistan Zero Point (BRTC Bus Counter).
- **Type**: Air Conditioned & Non-AC.
- **Fare**: ৳100 (Non-AC) / ৳150 (AC).
- **Best For**: Clean, comfortable family trips with guaranteed seats.

### 4. Shadhin Paribahan & Dholeshwari Paribahan
- **Boarding Point**: Mirpur 10 / Gabtoli via Babubazar Bridge to Mawa.
- **Fare**: ৳120 – ৳140.
- **Best For**: Passengers boarding directly from western Dhaka without going to Gulistan.

## ⏱️ Travel Time & Highway Highlights

- **Distance**: Approximately 38 km from Gulistan to Mawa Ghat.
- **Average Duration**: Only **35 to 50 minutes** via the expressway!
- **Key Attractions at Mawa**:
  - Fresh fried Hilsa (ইলিশ ভাজা) with mustard and hot rice at Mawa Ghat fish market.
  - Spectacular sunset views of the Padma Multipurpose Bridge.
  - Speedboat and river cruising on the mighty Padma River.

---

## 🌙 Return Trip: Mawa to Dhaka at Night

Buses from Mawa Ghat back to Dhaka operate until **11:00 PM**.
After 11:00 PM, CNG auto-rickshaws and shared microbuses are available directly to Gulistan and Jatrabari.
