# Bangladesh Intercity Bus Operators Directory

Comprehensive directory of top long-distance highway bus operators in Bangladesh, including Green Line, Hanif, Shyamoli, Shohagh, and Saintmartin Paribahan.

## Major Operators
- **Green Line Paribahan**: Premium Scania & Volvo Multi-Axle sleeper and chair cars.
- **Hanif Enterprise**: Largest non-AC and AC fleet connecting all 64 districts.
- **Shyamoli Paribahan**: Extensive domestic and cross-border (Dhaka-Kolkata-Siliguri) routes.
- **ENA Transport**: Dominant carrier for Greater Sylhet, Mymensingh, and North Bengal.
- **Saintmartin Hyundai**: Executive sleeper service to Cox's Bazar and Teknaf.
