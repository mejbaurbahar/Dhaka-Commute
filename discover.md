# Bangladesh Travel & Tourism Transport Guide

Detailed routing recommendations, bus options, train connections, and travel duration from Dhaka to Bangladesh's top 50+ tourist landmarks.

## Top Destinations
- **Cox's Bazar**: Longest natural sea beach in the world. Direct AC sleeper buses and Cox's Bazar Express / Parjotak Express train connections.
- **Sreemangal**: Tea capital of Bangladesh. Reached via Parabat Express, Jayantika Express, or highway buses (3.5–4.5 hours).
- **Sajek Valley**: Hill station in Rangamati. Highway bus to Khagrachhari, followed by 4x4 Chander Gari jeep convoy.
- **Saint Martin's Island**: Coral island accessible via cruise ships from Teknaf / Cox's Bazar.
