# Dhaka Metro Rail (MRT Line-6) Complete Commuter Guide

The Mass Rapid Transit Line-6 (MRT-6) is Dhaka's first elevated rapid transit rail system, operated by Dhaka Mass Transit Company Limited (DMTCL).

## Line Overview
- **Terminus Stations**: Uttara North (Diabari) to Motijheel
- **Total Active Stations**: 17 elevated stations
- **End-to-End Travel Duration**: 32 minutes (vs 90–150 minutes by road during peak congestion)
- **Operating Hours**: Saturday to Thursday: 07:10 AM – 09:40 PM; Friday: 03:00 PM – 09:40 PM.
- **Headway (Peak)**: 8–10 minutes

## Fare Structure & Ticketing
- **Minimum Fare**: ৳20 (covers adjacent 1-2 stations)
- **Maximum Fare**: ৳100 (Uttara North to Motijheel full traversal)
- **Rapid Pass / MRT Pass**: 10% discount on every journey (Maximum fare becomes ৳90).
- **Single Journey Token**: Available via ticket vending machines (TVM) at all stations.
