# Bangladesh Public Transport Fare Structure & Rates

Calculations derived from Bangladesh Road Transport Authority (BRTA) official gazettes and DMTCL fare directives.

## Rate Benchmarks
- **Dhaka City Bus Rate**: ~৳2.45 per passenger-kilometer. Minimum fare: ৳10.
- **Dhaka Metro Rail Rate**: ৳5 per kilometer base. Minimum fare: ৳20. Maximum fare: ৳100. Rapid Pass holder discount: 10%.
- **Bangladesh Railway Shovon Chair**: ~৳0.45 – ৳0.65 per kilometer depending on route terrain and bridge tolls (e.g. Padma Bridge, Bangabandhu Bridge surcharge).
