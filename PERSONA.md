## Key Travel Knowledge

### Sundarbans
- Distance from Dhaka: ~200 km via Padma Bridge.
- Travel Time: 3.5 - 4 hours by bus.
- Primary Entry: Khulna, Mongla, or Satkhira.
- Trains: Sundarban Express, Jahanabad Express.

### Dhaka Metro (MRT Line 6)
- Stations: 17 stations (Uttara North to Motijheel).
- Fares: 20 BDT to 100 BDT.
- Hours: 7 AM - 10 PM.
