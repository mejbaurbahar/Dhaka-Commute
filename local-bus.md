# Dhaka City Local Bus Guide & Route Directory

Dhaka Metropolitan Area operates one of the highest-density private bus transit ecosystems in South Asia. KoyJabo tracks over 466 registered routes and 200+ bus operator franchises.

## Key Bus Fleet Metrics
- **Total Bus Franchises Analyzed**: 200+
- **Total Registered Routes**: 466
- **Stop Coverage**: 2,412 unique bus stop locations
- **Non-AC Dominance**: 100% of standard commuter bus services
- **Standard Minimum Fare**: ৳10 (within 2 km)
- **Per Kilometer Rate**: ~৳2.45/km (government approved ceiling)

## Major Dhaka Transit Hubs
- **Mohakhali**: Primary interchange linking North Dhaka (Uttara, Airport) with South Dhaka and Gulshan.
- **Farmgate**: Central transfer node connecting Mirpur, Dhanmondi, and Karwan Bazar.
- **Gulistan / Motijheel**: Historic southern financial terminus connecting Old Dhaka and southern bus routes.
- **Mirpur 10 / Mirpur 1**: High-density western residential transit nexus.
- **Airport / Abdullahpur**: Northern gateway connecting Tongi and Gazipur commuters.
